About Me

I am a passionate Web Developer & Problem Solver

I’m Nazmul Hossain, a passionate CSE engineer and backend-focused developer from Bangladesh with strong experience in Python and Django. I enjoy building scalable web applications, management systems, and automation tools that solve real-world problems. My work includes student management platforms, room booking systems, result tracking systems, and API-driven applications. I am deeply interested in software architecture, cybersecurity, AI development, and modern web technologies. I believe in continuous learning, clean system design, and creating impactful digital solutions. Beyond coding, I enjoy exploring new technologies, contributing to projects, and turning innovative ideas into practical and user-friendly applications.
